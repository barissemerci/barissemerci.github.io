Privacy Policy

Water Eject · Last updated September 2026

Water Eject does not collect, store or share personal information about you. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

The app stores a single value locally: whether you have already seen the introduction screens. It never leaves your phone and is removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

Water Eject offers one optional in-app purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store; we never see or receive your payment details.

To recognise a purchase across your devices and to restore it when you reinstall, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated on your device, along with your purchase history and basic technical information such as device model, operating system version and country. This identifier is not linked to your name, email address or Apple ID.

Apple's handling of App Store transactions is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What the app does not access

  • No microphone, camera, photos or contacts
  • No location
  • No health, motion or biometric data
  • No advertising identifier

The app plays sound through your speaker. It does not record anything.

Children

Water Eject is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Because the app collects no personal information at all, this holds for every user.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. To have purchase records removed from RevenueCat, write to the address below and we will pass the request on.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
