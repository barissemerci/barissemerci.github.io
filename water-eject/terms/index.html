What the app does

Water Eject plays a low-frequency sound sweep through your phone's speaker. The vibration this creates can help push water droplets out of the speaker grille.

No guarantee of results

This is a physical technique with real limits. Unlike Apple Watch, an iPhone has no hardware designed for ejecting water, so the effect is partial and varies between devices. Water Eject may help; it cannot promise to remove water, restore muffled sound, or repair a device that has taken on water. It is not a substitute for professional service.

Use at your own risk

The app plays sound at high volume. Please note:

  • Never use it with headphones or earbuds connected. The app blocks this, but keep them unplugged anyway — sustained low-frequency sound at full volume can damage hearing.
  • If your device has been submerged or shows signs of internal water damage, contact Apple Support or an authorised service provider rather than relying on this app.
