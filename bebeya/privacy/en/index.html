Bebeya creates a fun, entertainment-only baby face image from the photos you upload. To do that, your photos are sent to a server. Below is exactly what goes where and for how long. No account, email or password is required.

What data is processed

  • Ultrasound photo (required) and optional mother / father photos: uploaded only after you choose them and give your consent.
  • The generated image and a record of the generation (time, status).
  • Anonymous ID: the app creates a random anonymous user ID on first launch. It isn't linked to your name, email or Apple ID.
  • Purchase information and your remaining generation count.

What happens to your photos

The ultrasound and parent photos you upload are sent to our server (Supabase, Frankfurt, EU) to be processed by image AI, and are deleted shortly after the generation finishes. Only the generated result image is kept in your history. No photo is uploaded without your consent.

To create the image, the photos are passed to an AI image model provider (Google Gemini). Photos are sent only for that generation.

Purchases

Payment is handled by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat is used to recognize and restore your purchase. RevenueCat receives the anonymous ID created on your device, your purchase history and basic technical information such as device model, OS version and country. Apple's handling of transactions is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Preventing abuse

Apple's DeviceCheck service is used to limit repeated use of the free preview. It doesn't provide an identifier for your device or any personal information.

Entertainment only

The generated image is for entertainment. It is not a medical prediction, a diagnosis, or a claim about how your baby will actually look.

Analytics and crash reports

To understand how Bebeya is used and to fix crashes, the app sends anonymous usage events (for example: generation started, succeeded or failed, paywall opened, purchase confirmed) and crash reports to Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) and Mixpanel. This data is linked only to your anonymous ID; it contains no photos, images or text you enter, and isn't used for advertising. Crash reports include technical details such as device model and OS version. Firebase Analytics runs without the advertising identifier, and Mixpanel doesn't derive location from your IP address. This data is sent only if you allow it: the app asks optionally on first launch, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Ads and tracking

Bebeya shows no ads, doesn't use the advertising identifier, and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Delete all my data removes the images and records linked to your account from the server. If you'd also like your purchase records removed from RevenueCat, write to the address below and we'll pass the request on.

Children

Bebeya isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
