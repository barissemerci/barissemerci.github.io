What the app does

DualShot photographs and films with the front and back cameras at the same time and combines the two frames into a single photo or video, which you can lay out as a picture-in-picture inset or a split.

Simultaneous use of both cameras is a hardware feature. It requires a recent iPhone; on a device that does not support it, the app will say so rather than pretend otherwise.

Free plan and subscription

DualShot can be used without paying. The free plan limits the number of shots per day, adds a small watermark to the result, keeps the output at a lower resolution, restricts video length, and offers only the picture-in-picture layout.

DualShot Pro removes all of those limits. It is offered as an auto-renewable subscription in two lengths:

  • Monthly — renews every month
  • Yearly — renews every year

The price for your region is shown inside the app before you confirm, and again by Apple at the point of purchase.

How the subscription renews

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before that period ends.

You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device, or from the subscription screen inside the app. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not refund the period already paid for.

Refunds are requested through Apple, not through us, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

You can restore an existing subscription on a new device from the subscription screen inside the app.

What happens to your shots

Shots you have already taken remain on your device, and remain yours, whether or not you subscribe. A watermark applied under the free plan stays on shots taken while that plan was in effect; subscribing removes it from new shots, not from old ones.

Deleting the app deletes the shots it holds. There is no copy anywhere else, so export anything you want to keep before you delete it.
