What the app does

Durak is a word puzzle game. You form words from a set of letters to complete levels, which are laid out as stops on fictional metro lines, and you can play one daily puzzle each day. The game plays in Turkish or English and works offline. The metro lines, stop names and colours in the game are invented and do not represent any real transit operator.

Playing for free

Durak can be played without paying. Free play includes ads: a full-screen ad between some levels, and an optional video you can watch to earn coins.

Coins

Coins are an in-game currency used for hints, such as revealing a letter or a word. You earn coins by finishing levels and daily puzzles, by finding bonus words and by watching optional videos, and you can also buy coin packs.

  • Coins have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money or transferred to anyone else, and can only be used inside Durak.
  • Coin packs are consumable purchases. Once delivered, they cannot be restored on another device or after the app is deleted.
  • Your coin balance is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes the balance.

Premium subscription

Durak Premium removes all ads and adds 50 coins to your balance every day you open the app. It is offered as an auto-renewable subscription in two lengths, without a free trial:

  • Weekly — renews every week
  • Yearly — renews every year

The price for your region is shown inside the app before you confirm, and again by Apple at the point of purchase.

How the subscription renews

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before that period ends.

You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not refund the period already paid for.

Refunds are requested through Apple, not through us, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

You can restore an existing subscription on a new device with "Restore purchases" in Durak's Settings.

Your progress

Your progress, streak and settings are stored on your device. There is no copy anywhere else, so deleting the app deletes them. New levels may be added in updates; the order and content of levels may change between versions.
