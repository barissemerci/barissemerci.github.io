Privacy Policy

Durak · Last updated October 2026

Durak does not ask who you are. There is no account to create and no analytics. Your game stays on your iPhone. Two services work alongside the app: RevenueCat, which handles purchases, and Google AdMob, which shows ads to players who have not bought Premium. This page explains what each of them receives.

Your game stays on your device

Your progress on each line, the levels and daily puzzles you have finished, your streak, your coin balance and your settings (game language, sound, haptics) are stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone. The word lists are built into the app, and the game works without an internet connection. We operate no servers that hold this information and have no way to see it.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, this data is included in that backup like any other app's data. That backup is handled by Apple or by your computer, not by us.

Purchases

Durak offers coin packs and an auto-renewable Premium subscription. Payment is handled by Apple through the App Store; we never see or receive your payment details.

To deliver purchases and recognise a subscription across your devices, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated on your device, along with your purchase history, basic technical information such as device model, operating system version and country, and whether the subscription screen was shown and closed. This identifier is not linked to your name, email address or Apple ID, and nothing about your game is sent with it.

Apple's handling of App Store transactions is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

If you have not bought Premium, Durak shows ads: a full-screen ad between some levels (never during your first ten levels, and not after every level), and a video you can choose to watch for coins. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

When an ad is requested, Google receives technical information it needs to deliver and measure the ad: your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and operating system information, the app's identifier and how you interact with the ad. Google uses this as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Before any ad is requested there, Durak shows Google's consent form, and ads are personalised only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in Durak's Settings under "Ad privacy choices".
  • Tracking permission. After that, iOS asks whether Durak may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you do not, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are still shown, just less tailored. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Premium. Premium players see no ads, and Durak does not request ads, ask for consent or ask for tracking permission for them.

Durak itself does not collect, store or sell any of this information. Ads never receive your progress, your words or your purchases.

What the app does not access

  • No location services
  • No camera, microphone or photo library
  • No contacts or calendar
  • No Health data
  • No account, email address or name

Children

Durak is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your progress, coins and settings from your device. To have purchase records removed from RevenueCat, write to the address below and we will pass the request on. Information Google receives when showing ads is governed by Google's own policies and controls, which you can reach from the links above.
