Privacy Policy

Aussie Citizen Prep · Last updated October 2026

Aussie Citizen Prep does not ask who you are. There is no account to create and no advertising. Your study progress stays on your iPhone. The app uses three outside services: RevenueCat, which handles purchases, and Google Firebase and Mixpanel, which tell us how the app is used and when it crashes. This page explains what each of them receives.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal information, and we run no servers that store data about you. The only information that leaves your iPhone is the usage, crash and purchase data described below.

What stays on your device

The following is saved only on your iPhone and is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your answers to practice questions and which questions you have mastered
  • Questions you have bookmarked
  • Your mock test results, scores and timings
  • A mock test in progress, and app settings such as having accepted the disclaimer

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Apple may include this data in the backup. That is handled by Apple, not by us.

Purchases

Aussie Citizen Prep offers optional Pro subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or payment details. To confirm whether you have Pro, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated on your device, your purchase history for the app and basic technical information such as the app version and device model. It is not linked to your name, email address or Apple Account by us. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Usage and crash data

To find out which features are used and to fix problems, the app sends anonymous events to Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) and to Mixpanel:

  • Simple actions, such as opening the app, opening a topic, starting or finishing a practice session or mock test (with the number of correct answers and whether it was passed), opening the subscription screen, and restoring or resetting.
  • Crash reports and performance information, including the device model, iOS version and app version.
  • An identifier generated for the app on your device, which cannot be used to identify you and is not linked to your name, email address or Apple Account.

The events never contain the questions you see, your answers or any text you type. The app does not use your advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites. Collection is off in development builds.

What the app does not access

  • Your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • Your Health data or calendar
  • Your advertising identifier

Children

The app is a study aid for adults preparing for the citizenship test and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Deleting your data

Choose Settings → Reset local progress in the app to delete your answer history, bookmarks and mock test results. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. Purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat are not stored by us; contact us if you would like help with a deletion request.

Not a government service

Aussie Citizen Prep is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Australian Government or the Department of Home Affairs. The official source for the citizenship test is the Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond booklet.
