Privacy Policy

Daymend · Last updated October 2026

Daymend does not collect personal information about you. There is no account to create, no advertising and no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites. What you tell the app — your goal, your pain areas, your history — stays on your iPhone. The app does send a small set of anonymous usage events and crash reports so we can see where people get stuck and fix what breaks; that is described below.

Your answers stay on your device

When you set up Daymend, it asks about your goal, where you feel pain or stiffness, how long you sit each day, where you work and how active you are, and, if you choose to give it, your first name. These answers are used only to build your plan. They are stored on your iPhone and are never sent to us, to RevenueCat or to anyone else. In particular, the body areas you mark as painful never leave your device.

Your history and settings

The routines you complete, the minutes you move, your streak, the days you mark as a day off, your reminder times and your chosen theme are all stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone. We operate no servers that hold this information and have no way to see it.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, this data is included in that backup like any other app's data. That backup is handled by Apple or by your computer, not by us.

Reminders and notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. No notification passes through a server of ours. Daymend asks for permission to send notifications, and works fully without it. The Start, Snooze and Skip buttons on a reminder are handled on the device.

To keep your upcoming reminders accurate, Daymend may briefly refresh its schedule in the background. This happens entirely on your device.

Live Activity

If you turn it on, a Live Activity shows your next break on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. The app and its Live Activity share a few settings, such as your theme and whether the Live Activity is on, through storage that belongs to Daymend on your device. You can turn it off in Daymend's settings or in iOS Settings.

Voice guidance

Spoken instructions during a routine are generated on your device by iOS. Daymend does not use the microphone and does not record anything.

Usage analytics and crash reports

To see which routines people actually finish, where they stop and why the app crashes, Daymend sends a small set of events to Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) and Mixpanel. The events are: onboarding started, each onboarding step completed, onboarding finished, the subscription screen shown, a purchase started, completed or restored, a routine started, completed or left unfinished, a reminder button used (Start, Snooze, Skip or Open), the notification permission allowed or declined, the theme changed, a day off switched on or off, and a streak milestone reached.

These events carry only the identifier of the routine or exercise, the moment of the day, a step name and plain counts such as seconds or days. They carry no name, no pain areas, no answers from the setup questions and no exercise history. Firebase also receives an app installation identifier, basic device and app information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate country, and, if the app crashes, the crash report with the stack trace. Mixpanel is configured not to use your IP address to determine your location.

We do not use Apple’s advertising identifier, we do not combine this data with data from other companies, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

Purchases

Daymend offers auto-renewable subscriptions. Payment is handled by Apple through the App Store; we never see or receive your payment details.

To recognise a subscription across your devices and restore it when you reinstall, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated on your device, along with your purchase history, basic technical information such as device model, operating system version and country, and whether the subscription screen was shown and closed. This identifier is not linked to your name, email address or Apple ID, and none of your answers, exercise history or health-related information is ever sent with it.

Apple's handling of App Store transactions is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What the app does not access

  • No location
  • No camera, microphone or photo library
  • No contacts or calendar
  • No Health app data
  • No advertising identifier

Children

Daymend is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from them, and the usage events described above carry nothing that identifies a person.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your answers, history and settings from your device. To have purchase records removed from RevenueCat, or the usage events and crash reports removed from Firebase and Mixpanel, write to the address below and we will pass the request on.
