What the app does

Daymend suggests short exercise routines for people who sit at a desk: micro breaks during work, seated workouts between tasks and longer routines after work. It builds a plan from the answers you give during setup and can remind you when it is time for a break.

Not medical advice

Daymend offers general wellness exercises. It is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Nothing in the app replaces advice from a doctor or physiotherapist.

Talk to a doctor or physiotherapist before starting if you are pregnant, are recovering from an injury or surgery, or have a heart, joint, back or other condition that exercise could affect. The pain areas you choose during setup shape your plan; they are not an assessment of your health.

Exercise safely

  • Move within a range that feels comfortable. Stop straight away if an exercise causes pain, dizziness, numbness or tingling.
  • Read the "Who should skip this" notes on each exercise and skip anything they describe.
  • Use a stable chair that cannot roll away and a desk that can take your weight, and clear the space around you.
  • See a doctor or physiotherapist about pain that does not go away.

You take part in every exercise at your own risk and are responsible for judging whether it is right for you.
